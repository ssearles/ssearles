## ⚠️ AI-assisted work

**The projects linked from this profile were built with AI assistance** — mostly
OpenCode working alongside Claude, on this machine, through this session.

They started from real problems rather than hypotheticals. The disk skills below
came out of a twelve-hour session rescuing a 2 TB backup drive that had stopped
mounting after a power cut. The AI did most of the writing and structuring; the
diagnosing, the verification, and the "that's still wrong, try again" was me.

Nothing here was published until I'd checked it against real hardware. I'd
rather publish something tested and slightly rough than polished and untested.

## Projects

### [disk-backup-skills](https://github.com/ssearles/disk-backup-skills)

Agent skills covering external USB disk diagnostics and a Rescuezilla backup
workflow — written for AI coding agents, but the knowledge is real.

They document the traps that cost most of that troubleshooting session: why
SMART self-tests abort on cheap USB bridges, the three different causes of a
read-only mount and their three different fixes, and how to verify a copy
without drowning in false mismatches.
